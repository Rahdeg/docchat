Your documents stay yours

  • Private by default. Files are only reachable through your signed-in account, never by a public link.
  • Delete means delete. Remove a document, or your whole account, and its text, passages and chats go with it.
  • Documents are data, not instructions. Text hidden inside a file can't make DocChat do anything you didn't ask for.
