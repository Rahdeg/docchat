Rent increase notice
How much notice does my landlord need to give before raising the rent?

At least one month's notice in writing. The notice must say what the new rent is and the date it starts 1.

Your landlord can only raise the rent once during the fixed term, not before six months, and by no more than CPI plus 1% 2.
