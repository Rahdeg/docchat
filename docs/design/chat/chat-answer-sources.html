How much notice does my landlord need to give before raising the rent?

At least one month's notice in writing. The notice must say what the new rent is and the date it starts .

There are limits on the increase too. Your landlord can only raise the rent once during the fixed term, not before six months have passed, and by no more than the CPI rate plus 1% .

If a notice doesn't follow these rules, you can write asking for it to be withdrawn, and your current rent stays the same until a valid notice arrives .
